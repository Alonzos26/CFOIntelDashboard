# Source-to-feature and formula mapping

> This document describes each source in the source's own terms. The product shows some measures under its own names: core capital appears as **operating reserve**, the four forces of cash flow as the **Cash Flow Waterfall**, profit levels as **profit tiers**, economic value as **estimated business value**, and labor efficiency (LER) as the **Labor Yield Ratio (LYR)**. The full table is in the README.

What was found in the attached workbooks and workshop decks, what the application does with it, where the sources disagree, and what was corrected. Cell references are to the attached files. Status: **P1** = built in Phase 1, **P2/P3** = designed for, not yet built.

## 2. Where the workbooks conflict — and what the application does

| # | Conflict | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Profitability basis.** Deluxe Dashboard grades *reported* R12 profit (D10 → `Rolling 12!AH38`, 7.3 %) while its own true profit is negative (AH52, −3.3 %). Prime shows both ("Actuals" and "True Profit"). | The scorecard grades **true (adjusted) profit** and displays reported profit beside it. |
| 2 | **Payroll taxes and benefits.** The P&L format keeps them in operating expenses and the salary cap subtracts them as a non-labor cost, so "total labor" excludes burden. The brief asks for tLER on total labor cost. | Per-client setting `burdenTreatment`: `opex` (source method, default) or `labor`. Burden is counted exactly once either way; a test proves headroom is identical under both. |
| 3 | **Owner pay in two places.** Profit Needs treats "Owner's Distributions/Pay" as a use of profit; the CF model treats owner pay as a market wage inside labor. Using both double-counts. | Market-rate pay is a labor cost (via the owner adjustment). The Profit Planner asks only for distributions **beyond** salary. |
| 4 | **Core capital basis.** `BS!59` uses the single latest month, so the target jumps with seasonality. Prime's commentary quotes a different figure than its sheet. | Configurable basis: 12-month average (default), 3-month average, or latest month (source). See also row 10 for what the spend includes. |
| 5 | **Cash-flow column offset.** In `CF`, A/R change is `BS!C9 − BS!D9` (this month minus next) while A/P is `BS!C30 − BS!B30` (this minus prior); `KPI!B10` pulls `CF!C21`. Cash flow is shifted one month against the P&L. | All deltas are current minus prior for the same month. |
| 6 | **Interest.** Hard-coded 7 % of notes + LOC (`PL by Month!34`), overriding actual interest. | Actual interest is imported. A rate assumption belongs to P2 forecasting and will be configurable. |
| 7 | **"Rolling 12" with fewer than 12 months.** The first eleven columns sum whatever exists and are still labelled R12. | Windows carry `complete` and `monthsIncluded`; partial windows are labelled and excluded from R12 trend lines. |
| 8 | **Weeks per month**: 52/12 in one calculator, 4.33 in another. | Annual hours × rate ÷ 12 throughout. |
| 10 | **What counts in core capital.** The book's example sets the target at two months of operating expenses ($100,000 for a $1M business), which excludes non-labor cost of goods sold. The SJATS workbooks (`BS!59`) and the Prime commentary include COGS. | **Decided by SJATS: the book's definition is the default** — two months of operating expenses and direct labor, COGS excluded. The workbook version remains a per-client option. |
| 11 | **Tax set-aside.** The Profit Planning Worksheet example uses 25%; the book's example uses 40%; neither reflects a client's actual tax planning. | Per-client planning rate (default 40%) used until the advisor enters a **projected tax for the year from tax planning**; that projection then replaces rate × profit and is reserved in full, less taxes already paid. |
| 9 | **Gross profit definition.** The Profit Plan example puts all payroll below gross profit; the management format separates direct labor. | The management format governs. Mapping decides which wage accounts are direct. |

## 3. Assumptions that are configurable (per client unless noted)

Target pretax profit %; direct and management LYR targets; sales target; operating-expense % target; DSO target and window (30/60/90 days); core capital months, basis and COGS inclusion; burden treatment; weekly gross-profit target; minimum cash; fiscal-year start; week-start day; every grade threshold; whether clients may build scenarios. In scenarios: every payroll tax rate and wage base, workers' comp %, benefits, PTO, one-time costs, training and ramp months, margin on new revenue, collection lag (from DSO), cash guardrail. In the KPI library: every formula, unit, direction, target and alert level. **No benchmark ships as a default** — a missing target shows "No target set" and the grade is N/A.
